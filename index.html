Wand Test

Your phone is the wand. Foes walk up one at a time. Cast at them with your wrist and listen for what happens.

  • Fireball — push the phone straight forward and stop.
  • Lightning — chop down, tipping the top of the phone away from you.
  • Frost — twist your wrist like turning a key.
  • Ward — sweep the phone left or right. Cast it when you hear the rising warning tone to block the attack.

Hold the phone upright with the screen facing you. Each spell has its own sound and buzz, so you can cast without looking. A foe's weak spell hits twice as hard and breaks its wind-up. A hollow sliding sound means the gesture wasn't recognised.

Grip it firmly and keep clear of people and furniture. Vibration works on Android; iPhones don't let web pages vibrate.
